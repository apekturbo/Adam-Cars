This project currently loads car photos directly from Unsplash (see the
image URLs near the top of script.js, in CAR_DATA / FEATURED_DATA) so the
site works instantly with no setup.

To use your own photos instead:
1. Save your images into assets/images/ (car photos) or assets/icons/
   (logo, small icons).
2. In script.js, change each "img" value from an https:// URL to a local
   path, e.g. "assets/images/civic.jpg".
3. Recommended size for car photos: at least 1200px wide, 4:3 or 16:10
   ratio, under 400KB each (compress with squoosh.app or tinypng.com)
   for fast loading.
